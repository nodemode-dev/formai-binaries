The OpenCascade wheel also bundles image libraries (among them libraw, libfreeimage, libjasper,
libjpeg, libpng, libtiff, libwebp, freetype). Their licences and source are listed in
SOURCES.md of the release; the worker uses none of them for FormCAD files.

All LGPL libraries are shipped as separate shared files in the library folder next to the
program and may be replaced by your own build of the same libraries.
